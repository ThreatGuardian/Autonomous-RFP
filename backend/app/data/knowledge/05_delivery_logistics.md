# Delivery and logistics

## Delivery lead times
In-stock items are dispatched within two to five business days of a confirmed purchase order. Items sourced from distribution typically ship within seven to fourteen days; build-to-order servers and network equipment may take up to four weeks. Confirmed dates are provided with order acknowledgement.

## International shipping
International consignments are shipped by air freight through our logistics partners with full insurance and tracking. Unless otherwise agreed, international quotations are prepared on a Delivered Duty Paid basis, meaning Meridian manages export clearance, freight, import clearance and destination taxes.

## Packaging and sustainability
Equipment is shipped in manufacturer packaging consolidated on pallets to reduce waste. Packaging is removed and recycled at deployment through certified recyclers.

## Partial deliveries
Where some items are on extended lead time, Meridian can ship in phases so that in-stock equipment reaches the client first. Partial delivery is invoiced proportionally.
