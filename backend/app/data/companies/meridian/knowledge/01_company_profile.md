# Company profile

## About Meridian Systems
Meridian Systems & Supply is an IT infrastructure partner headquartered in Pune, India, serving education, healthcare, logistics and professional-services organisations since 2011. We supply, deploy and support end-user computing, networking, data-centre and collaboration equipment for clients in India, the Gulf, Singapore, the United Kingdom, Europe and North America.

## Scale and track record
Our team of 64 includes 22 certified engineers and a dedicated bid and project-management office. Over the past three financial years we have delivered more than 410 projects and shipped over 38,000 devices, with an on-time delivery rate of 97.2 percent and a customer satisfaction score of 4.7 out of 5.

## Partner credentials
Meridian holds Dell Technologies Titanium Partner, HP Amplify Gold, Lenovo Platinum, Cisco Premier Integrator, Fortinet Advanced Partner and Microsoft Solutions Partner (Modern Work) credentials. Direct distributor relationships give us authorised pricing, genuine stock and full manufacturer warranty on every unit supplied.

## Why clients choose us
Clients choose Meridian because a single accountable team owns the full lifecycle: sizing, procurement, staging, deployment, support and eventual IT asset disposal. Every project is assigned a named project manager and a named support lead who remain the client's point of contact after go-live.
