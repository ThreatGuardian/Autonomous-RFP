# Warranty programmes

## Manufacturer warranty
All hardware is supplied new, in original sealed packaging, with full manufacturer warranty registered in the client's name. Warranty periods vary by product line and are listed against each item in the quotation.

## Extended warranty
Meridian extended warranty adds twelve or twenty-four months beyond the manufacturer term. Coverage includes parts, labour and next-business-day replacement of failed components. Claims are raised through a single Meridian service desk; the client never needs to deal with multiple manufacturers.

## Accidental damage protection
Accidental damage protection covers drops, liquid spills and screen breakage on notebooks. One claim per device per year is included, with a repair turnaround target of five business days and a loan device available for critical users.

## Advance replacement
For servers, storage and core network equipment under Meridian support, failed units are replaced in advance: the replacement ships before the faulty unit is returned, minimising downtime.
