# Commercial terms

## Validity
Prices in this quotation are valid for thirty days from the date of issue, subject to stock availability at the time of purchase order.

## Payment terms
Standard payment terms are thirty days from invoice for approved accounts. Public-sector and education clients may request milestone-based payment. International orders are invoiced in the quoted currency; payment may be made by bank transfer or letter of credit.

## Currency and exchange
Where a quotation is issued in a currency other than Indian rupees, prices are converted at the prevailing mid-market rate on the quotation date. Meridian holds the quoted price for the validity period, absorbing exchange movements up to three percent.

## Taxes
Taxes are shown separately in the quotation according to the delivery jurisdiction. For domestic supply within India, GST is applied as CGST and SGST for intra-state supply and IGST for inter-state supply. For international supply, destination indirect taxes are applied as applicable to the incoterm.

## Warranty and returns
Dead-on-arrival units reported within seven days are replaced at no cost. Other returns are subject to manufacturer policy.
