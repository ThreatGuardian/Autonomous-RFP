# Certifications and compliance

## Quality management
Meridian is certified to ISO 9001:2015 for the supply, integration and support of IT infrastructure. Our processes are audited annually by an accredited certification body.

## Information security
Meridian is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022. Client data handled during imaging, migration or support is processed under documented controls, encrypted in transit and at rest, and deleted on project closure.

## Environmental management
Meridian is certified to ISO 14001:2015. We operate an e-waste take-back programme under authorised recyclers and provide certificates of destruction for retired storage media.

## Data protection
We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 in India and support clients' obligations under GDPR and UK GDPR, including data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses for cross-border transfers.

## Accessibility and product standards
All supplied equipment carries the relevant BIS, CE, FCC or UKCA markings for the destination market, and energy-rated products meet ENERGY STAR or EPEAT criteria where available.
