# Support and managed services

## Service desk
The Meridian service desk operates from 08:00 to 20:00 IST, Monday to Saturday, reachable by phone, e-mail and portal. Priority-one incidents on covered infrastructure are acknowledged within 30 minutes and receive a remote engineer within four hours.

## Next-business-day on-site support
Next-business-day on-site support provides a certified engineer at the client location on the business day following diagnosis. It covers servers, storage, switching, wireless, firewalls and UPS systems and includes firmware advisory and quarterly health checks.

## Service level reporting
Clients receive a monthly service report covering incident volumes, response and resolution times against target, open problems and upcoming firmware or end-of-life notices.

## Escalation matrix
Every contract includes a named escalation matrix: support lead, service delivery manager and a director. Escalations are reviewed within one business day.
