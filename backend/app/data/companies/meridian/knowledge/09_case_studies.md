# Case studies

## Engineering college campus refresh
For an engineering college in Pune, Meridian replaced 620 laboratory desktops and 140 faculty notebooks over a four-week summer window, staged and imaged off site, with zero teaching days lost.

## Hospital network modernisation
For a 300-bed hospital group in Karnataka, Meridian designed and deployed a segmented Cisco network with 180 Wi-Fi 6 access points and redundant FortiGate firewalls, cutting over ward by ward with no clinical downtime.

## Logistics company regional rollout
For a freight forwarder in Dubai, Meridian delivered notebooks, docking stations and video-conferencing rooms across four sites on a delivered-duty-paid basis, with a single support contract covering all locations.

## Professional-services firm cloud migration
For an advisory firm in Singapore, Meridian migrated 3.2 TB of mailbox and file data to Microsoft 365, deployed endpoint protection and trained administrators, completing the project in six weeks.
