Subject: Supply of computing and networking equipment for the new Computer Science block

1. Background
Sahyadri Institute of Technology is commissioning a new Computer Science block with four teaching laboratories and a faculty wing. The Institute invites sealed quotations from authorised partners for the equipment listed below.

2. Schedule of requirements
- 60 x Business laptops for faculty, Intel Core i5 or better, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, 14 inch display
- 120 desktop computers (tower), Core i5, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, Windows 11 Pro
- 120 nos. 24-inch Full HD monitors with height adjustable stand
- 48-port PoE+ managed switches with Layer 3 support: Qty 4
- Wi-Fi 6 ceiling access points (24 units)
- Online UPS 3kVA - 4
- 30 boxes of Cat6 cable, 305 m
- 120 wireless keyboard and mouse combos

3. Delivery and installation
All items must be delivered to the Narhe campus, Pune within 30 days of the purchase order. Installation and configuration of all devices is to be included.

4. Warranty
All end-user devices must carry a minimum 3 years comprehensive onsite warranty.

5. Commercial terms
Prices must be quoted in INR, inclusive of GST shown separately. Payment will be made within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice. No advance payment will be released.

6. Evaluation
The contract will be awarded to the lowest compliant bidder (L1) among technically qualified vendors.

7. Submission
Quotations must be submitted by 10 October 2026 to purchase@sahyadri.edu.in.
