REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
RFP #: CVUSD-TECH-27-003
Title: Classroom technology and wireless refresh
Release date: 09/15/2026

The District invites proposals for classroom technology serving 14 schools. Pricing should be quoted in US dollars.

Items requested
- 150 student laptops, Core i5, 8GB RAM, 512GB SSD
- 40 x 27 inch 4K monitors, USB-C
- Wi-Fi 6 access points: Qty 60
- 24-port PoE switches, smart managed - 12
- 20 network laser printers
- 12 interactive whiteboards, 75 inch

Delivery shall be made to the District warehouse in Fresno within 45 days. Freight must be included.
The District requires a minimum 3 year warranty on all hardware.
Proposals will be scored: price 50%, support 30%, experience 20%.
Proposals are due by 10/20/2026 at 3:00 PM PT to purchasing@cedarvalley.k12.ca.us.
