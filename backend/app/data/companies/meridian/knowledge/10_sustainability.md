# Sustainability

## Responsible sourcing
We prioritise products with EPEAT Gold or Silver ratings and energy-efficient power supplies, and we report estimated lifetime energy use for large desktop and monitor deployments on request.

## IT asset disposition
Meridian collects retired equipment, performs certified data sanitisation to NIST 800-88, and refurbishes or recycles devices through authorised partners. Clients receive an asset-level disposal report.

## Carbon-aware logistics
Consolidated shipping and route planning reduce the number of deliveries per project. Where the client requests it, we provide an estimate of logistics emissions for the order.
